## Answer keys and selection

I selected reports from independently designed utility publications, excluding fixed state-agency templates. A fixed-seed random draw chose the held-out reports from the collected pool, with no water system shared between the examples and evaluation. The final evaluation set contains 20 systems in 20 states.

Each key was built from two independent readings against the task's output format. I resolved disagreements on the page image and excluded selected reports whose interpretation the rules could not settle, or whose results tables existed only as images. The JSON keys were created for the benchmark; only the PDFs came from the publishers.

Reports whose sole contact was a named individual at a private address or a free-mail account were removed during collection. Public-role contacts remain in the retained documents. The reports have no blanket public-domain designation, and this register grants no reuse permission. Publishers and other rights holders can request removal through the procedure in [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md).
