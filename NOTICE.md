# Notice on third-party documents in this repository

**The Consumer Confidence Reports are third-party publications. No reuse licence was found in the PDFs.** They were downloaded from public utility, municipal and regional water-board websites and are included unedited for document-extraction research. [DATA-SOURCES.md](DATA-SOURCES.md) lists every report, publisher, source URL and checksum prefix.

Where they are used:

- `tasks/water-report-extraction/environment/data/visible/`: four example PDFs, each supplied with a benchmark answer key.
- `tasks/water-report-extraction/tests/hidden/`: twenty evaluation PDFs from other water systems.

Any copyright remains with the water systems and other contributors who produced the reports. This repository claims no ownership of the PDFs and grants no rights to them. Their availability on public websites does not supply a reuse licence.

Some reports name utility staff and officials in their public roles. The collection review excluded reports whose sole contact was a named person at a private address or a free-mail account.

A water system or other rights holder may request removal by opening a repository issue or contacting me on GitHub, [@nadiaayaz](https://github.com/nadiaayaz). The requested document will be removed.

I created the answer keys, task-specific code, instructions and evaluation records for this benchmark. Those materials are separate from the third-party reports covered by this notice.
